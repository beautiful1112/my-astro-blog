# Design exercise

[← Module index](README.md) · [↑ Multicast master index](../Multicast_Deep_Dive.md)

---

Design delivery for two exchange cross-connects, two network fabrics, 40 receiver servers in two rooms, 300 groups, and 5 Mpps aggregate peak. A single link, switch, or NIC failure must not interrupt the normalized book.

Specify group/source allocation, L2/L3 boundaries, SSM/ASM, queriers, RPF topology, A/B arbitration, recovery, QoS, capacity, time synchronization, security, and evidence proving independence.
