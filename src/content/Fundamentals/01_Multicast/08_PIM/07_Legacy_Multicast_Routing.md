# Legacy multicast routing protocols

[← Module index](README.md) · [↑ Multicast master index](../Multicast_Deep_Dive.md)

---

- **DVMRP:** early distance-vector flood-and-prune protocol.
- **MOSPF:** OSPF extension computing multicast trees from link-state membership.
- **CBT:** historical core-based shared-tree protocol.
- **PIM-SM/SSM:** dominant modern enterprise/data-center models.
- **PIM-DM:** still encountered, but generally unsuitable for sparse high-bandwidth distribution.

In interviews, explain the traffic-density and state trade-off rather than only naming protocols.
