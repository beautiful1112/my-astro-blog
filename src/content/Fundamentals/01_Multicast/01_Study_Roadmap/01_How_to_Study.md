# How to study multicast

[← Module index](README.md) · [↑ Multicast master index](../Multicast_Deep_Dive.md)

---

Use three passes:

1. Build the mental model: packet flow, service models, membership, Layer 2, and RPF.
2. Master operations: predict control-plane state, interpret captures, and localize loss.
3. Prepare for interviews: solve the practical cases and labs without looking at the answers, then use the recall sheets.

Whenever platform syntax differs, preserve the invariant being tested: membership, Layer-2 replication, RPF, multicast routing state, packet counters, or application sequence state.
