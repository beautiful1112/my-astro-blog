# BGP's Role in Quantitative-Trading Infrastructure

BGP commonly appears at:

- Internet and DDoS-provider edges.
- Private exchange, broker, and market-data interconnects.
- WAN and cloud connectivity.
- Data-center EVPN fabrics.
- MPLS/VPN segmentation.
- Anycast services.

BGP chooses reachability according to policy; it does not natively optimize exchange-to-strategy latency.

A good trading design translates business intent—venue priority, circuit class, deterministic failover, and security boundary—into explicit policy and verifies the data plane continuously.
