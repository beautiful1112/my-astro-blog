# Interview: security and automation

## Q1 — Fail-open or fail-closed NAC?

**Model:** Availability vs confidentiality; write RTO. Hospitals often phased/open on IdP loss; PCI enclave may close.

## Q2 — Segmentation vs encryption?

**Model:** Encryption is confidentiality in flight; segmentation is who can reach. You often need both.

## Q3 — CI/CD blast radius?

**Model:** Canaries and rollback; global push is a weapon.

## Q4 — Visibility vs assurance?

**Model:** Seeing counters vs proving intent matches state.

## Q5 — AI egress?

**Model:** Treat external models as untrusted partners: DLP, allow-list, residency.
