# Misconception: cloud removes network design

## The myth

“Once it is in the cloud, networking is the provider’s problem.”

## Why it is wrong

You still design identity, egress, region, hybrid seams, encryption, inspection, and cost of data transfer. SaaS still has an experience and residency problem.

## Counterexample

Default-route-to-HQ for all cloud traffic: 2010 WAN with a 2026 invoice.

## Correct habit

Place the workload, then design the path and PEP.
