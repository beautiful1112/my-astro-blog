# Misconception: HLD is just a pretty drawing

## The myth

“HLD is visio; real work is LLD/CLI.”

## Why it is wrong

HLD is where failure domains, policy points, and business fit are decided. A wrong HLD makes perfect LLD a precise implementation of a bad system.

## Counterexample

Beautiful interface spreadsheet on a single-hub WAN that violates site-loss RTO.

## Correct habit

HLD first; LLD only to discriminate options or to implement.
