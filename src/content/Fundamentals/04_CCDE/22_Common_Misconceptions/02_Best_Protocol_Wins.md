# Misconception: the best protocol wins

## The myth

“IS-IS/BGP/SD-WAN/EVPN is always the expert choice.”

## Why it is wrong

CCDE scores **fit to R/C/A**. A two-person shop with Cisco EIGRP and a 30-day merger deadline may lose if you impose IS-IS+SR for prestige.

## Counterexample

Greenfield SP core: IS-IS may win. Same engineer’s retail WAN: EIGRP stub or SD-WAN may win.

## Correct habit

“Best” = operable under this constraint set.
