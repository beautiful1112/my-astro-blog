# User and application experience

CCDE v3.1 adds UX as a design topic. The network is good if **the application meets its contract**, not if all links are green.

Design from SLOs: voice MOS/jitter, transaction latency, job completion, join time on Wi-Fi.

Tools: app-aware SD-WAN, WAN optimization only if the app still needs it, QoS, local breakout, anycast, Internet quality probes.

A perfect IGP with a 400 ms SaaS hairpin is a failed UX design.

## Interview framing

“I take the application SLO as a requirement and I will break a pretty topology to meet it—local breakout, QoS, or a closer region.”
