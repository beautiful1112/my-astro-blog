# Blueprint domain card (v3.1)

| Domain | Weight | Remember |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Business strategy | 15% | Method, RPO/ROI/cost, sustainability, AI/ML, sovereignty |
| 2 Planes and ops | 25% | Flow, planes, central/hybrid, automation, SD, observability, UX |
| 3 Network design | 30% | Modular resilient secure; constraints; migration; AI use cases |
| 4 Service design | 15% | Apps on IP; cloud/hybrid placement and connect |
| 5 Security design | 15% | Segment, NAC, assurance, PEP, CIA, compliance, AI policy |

If an option ignores business/security but is “better OSPF,” it is still wrong.

Official topics: [Cisco CCDE v3.1](https://learningnetwork.cisco.com/s/ccde-v3-1-unified-exam-topics).
