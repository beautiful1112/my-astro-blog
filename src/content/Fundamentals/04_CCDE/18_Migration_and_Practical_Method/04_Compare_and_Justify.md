# Compare and justify

For each decision, force **two options**.

```text
Decision: campus L2/L3 boundary
A: L3 access
B: L2 access + vPC dist
Win: A because R1 (loop last year) + scale of buildings
Cost: more IGP (ops already Cisco, acceptable)
Discard B: larger STP domain violates R1
```

If both meet R/C/A, pick the one that is **easier to operate or migrate**, and say that. “Both work” is not an answer; “both work, I pick A because ops skill is a constraint” is.

## Interview framing

“Every choice has a loser. I write why the loser lost against a numbered requirement.”
