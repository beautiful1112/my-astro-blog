# Practical time and traps

## Time

- Modules 1–3 are core; do not bleed all energy before the elective.
- Capture R/C/A fast; do not over-draw pretty LLD.
- If stuck, pick a defensible option and **move**—inconsistency later is worse than a slightly coarse HLD now.

## Traps

- Solving a protocol lab the scenario did not ask
- Ignoring a late business change
- Stretching L2 because VMs “might” move
- Putting the Internet table in the IGP
- Single controller/identity with a tight RTO
- Gold-plating a site that the budget called cheap
- Forgetting IPv6
- Forgetting operations (who runs this at 03:00)

Elective: only study that list in depth; still win 1–3 on core enterprise.

## Interview framing

“On Practical I budget time for the story changes, I keep an R/C/A table, and I refuse L2 stretch, IGP Internet, and silent assumptions.”
