# How to read a scenario

Practical scenarios are **long and they change**. First pass: inventory, not solutions.

1. Draw sites, DC, WAN, Internet, cloud as boxes.
2. List applications and their contracts.
3. Mark existing protocols (constraints).
4. Highlight numbers (RTO, users, prefixes, budget, dates).
5. Note politics (who operates, merger).
6. Leave space: later modules will add/change requirements.

Do not fall in love with a technology in paragraph one. Module 3 may forbid it.

## Interview framing

“I read a scenario as a living inventory of R/C/A. I do not pick a favorite protocol until the constraints are on one page.”
